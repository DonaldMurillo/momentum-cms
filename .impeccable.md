## Design Context

### Users

Content editors, developers, and marketing teams who use Momentum CMS as their daily content workspace. Sessions are long (30+ min), the work is focused, and the tool needs to feel respected — not cute, not playful, not aggressively branded. Users are professionals; they notice when typography is sloppy and when spacing is uniform-and-boring.

### Brand Personality

**Considered. Editorial. Confident.** Three words: _refined, precise, opinionated_. The product should feel like a tool a senior craftsperson would choose — closer in spirit to Linear, Notion's restraint, and NYT product than to Vercel's neon or Supabase's cyan-on-dark. Voice in copy can be lightly tsundere/dry, but the visual language is calm and deliberate.

### Aesthetic Direction

**"Editorial Workshop"** — typographically driven, generously spaced where it earns it, dense where data demands it. Warm-tinted neutrals (paper + ink, not flat gray). One confident accent: **deep ultramarine**, used rarely. No gradients, no glassmorphism, no glow, no hero metric tiles, no side-stripe accent borders, no decorative icon-above-heading patterns.

References: Linear, Notion, NYT product, Vercel docs (the calm parts), Stripe Dashboard's typographic discipline.
Anti-references: Supabase cyan-on-dark, Material You's playful curves, generic shadcn/ui blue palette, dashboard-template tile grids.

### Design Principles

1. **Typography does the work, not chrome.** Hierarchy comes from weight, size, and tracking — not from background pills, dividers, or boxes everywhere.
2. **Tinted neutrals over flat gray.** Every neutral leans warm toward the brand. No `#fff` or `#000`.
3. **Vary the spacing.** Tight where information clusters, generous where headings need air. Same padding everywhere = monotonous.
4. **One accent, used rarely.** Ultramarine appears for primary actions, focus rings, and the occasional emphasis — that's it. Rarity is what makes it work.
5. **No motion theater.** View transitions enabled at document level for free progressive enhancement; no spinners, bouncy reveals, or animated micro-interactions.

### Type System

- UI/body: **Public Sans** (Google Fonts) — variable font, professional grotesk used by US Web Design System. Distinct from Inter/Geist. Weights 300–700.
- Mono: **Recursive Mono Casual** (Google Fonts) — variable, distinctive humanist mono for IDs, code, field keys.
- Loaded via Google Fonts CSS in `index.html`, no bundle cost beyond standard webfont request.
- Banned (impeccable reflex list): Inter, IBM Plex, Geist, Fraunces, DM Sans, Plus Jakarta, Space Grotesk, Outfit.

### Color System (OKLCH)

- Paper (light bg): `oklch(0.99 0.005 75)` — warm off-white
- Ink (dark fg / dark bg): `oklch(0.18 0.012 75)` — warm near-black
- Accent (ultramarine): `oklch(0.42 0.18 265)`
- Success (sage): `oklch(0.55 0.10 145)`
- Danger (clay): `oklch(0.55 0.16 25)`
- Warning (amber): `oklch(0.72 0.13 70)`

### Theme

**Default: system.** Light mode is the canonical experience (long content sessions, media review, comparison with web output). Dark mode must be equally polished — not an afterthought.
